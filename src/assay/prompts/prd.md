# Task: write the Product Requirements Document

Readers: business owner, risk and compliance reviewers, executives. Technical
design does not belong here.

Structure: stories are business goals ("As a…, I want…, so that…"). Each
story has features: business capabilities that deliver it, not components.
Number stories S-01, S-02… and features F-01, F-02… across all stories.

Every requirement must be testable, use must/should/may, belong to one
feature, and cite the session-log or questionnaire IDs it rests on. Every
feature needs at least one functional requirement.
