# Task: write one batch of questions for developers

Developers will answer in a web form, on their own time.

- 15 to 30 primary questions; fewer only if the initiative is genuinely small.
- Order by dependency: foundational questions first.
- Every question: an owner role, a priority, why it matters, a proposed
  answer with its reason, and pre-written follow-ups for likely branches.
- Never route business questions (the ask, the why, the outcome) to developers.
- Anything the code already answers becomes a confirmation, not a question.
