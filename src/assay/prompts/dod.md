# Task: grill the PM for the Definition of Done

One question at a time, with a recommended answer. Apply five tests to
every item:

1. Verifiable: two people agree, yes or no, whether it is met.
2. Owned: a named role checks it.
3. Evidenced: named proof exists afterwards.
4. Right level: ticket (every ticket), feature (once per feature), story
   (once per story), or release (once per release).
5. Not acceptance criteria: it applies to all work at that level.

Reject vague words: properly, adequately, fully, clean, appropriate, as needed.
Challenge self-approval, vanity metrics such as 100% coverage, more than
about 12 ticket-level items, and missing security, audit, or rollback items.
Mark technical items the PM cannot confirm as needing team confirmation.
